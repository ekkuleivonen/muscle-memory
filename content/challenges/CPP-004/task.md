Return a new sequence rotated to the right by k positions.

Implement `std::vector<int> rotate_right(const std::vector<int>& values, int k)`. k is 0..10000 and may exceed the length. Empty input stays empty; preserve duplicates and do not change the input. At most 100 integers, each in -10000..10000.

## Examples

Input: `[[1, 2, 3, 4], 1]` → output: `[4, 1, 2, 3]`.
Input: `[[], 7]` → output: `[]`.
